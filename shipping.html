Shipping & Delivery

Effective 3 September 2026 · Last updated 3 September 2026

ATAV POS is an online software service. We do not ship physical goods, parcels, or POS hardware as part of the subscription collected through Cashfree.

What is “delivered”

Delivery means we activate or extend your shop account on https://pos.atavtelecom.in so you can sign in and use the POS in a browser.

When you get access

  • Signup / trial: the shop is created immediately after you submit Create business on the login page. You are signed in as business admin.
  • Paid subscription: after Cashfree confirms a successful payment, paid access is usually enabled within a few minutes. In rare gateway delays it can take up to 24 hours. Email noc@atavtelecom.in if access is still missing after payment.

How you receive it

No courier is used. Sign-in is at login.html using the email, username, or mobile and password you set. Transactional email (welcome / payment) may be sent from pos@atavtelecom.in.

Service area

The Service is offered to businesses in India. You need a working internet connection and a current browser. We do not provide installation media or postal delivery of the software.

Failed delivery

If payment succeeded but the shop is not unlocked, treat it as a delivery failure and contact support with the Cashfree reference. See Refund & Cancellation if we cannot restore access.
