Privacy Policy

Effective 3 September 2026 · Last updated 3 September 2026

ATAV Telecom (“ATAV”, “we”, “us”) operates the ATAV POS multi-tenant point-of-sale software at https://pos.atavtelecom.in (the “Service”). This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you create a shop, sign in, use the POS, or pay a subscription through Cashfree.

Who we are

ATAV Telecom, Pune, Maharashtra, India. Support: noc@atavtelecom.in · +91 97650 40588.

Information we collect

  • Account and shop data: business name, type, category, owner name, mobile, email, address, city, state, PIN, GSTIN, PAN, logo, admin username, and password (stored as a hash).
  • Operational POS data: items, customers, suppliers, bills, holds, purchases, expenses, reports, staff users, and settings you enter while using the shop.
  • Technical data: browser type, IP address, device, login time, and error logs needed to keep the Service secure.
  • Payment data: subscription plan, amount, GST, invoice status, and Cashfree payment reference. We do not collect or store full card numbers, UPI PINs, or CVV. Card and UPI data is processed by Cashfree Payments India Private Limited on their secure checkout.

How we use information

  • To create and run your shop POS, authenticate users, and provide support.
  • To start a 2-day trial, collect subscription fees, issue receipts, and prevent fraud.
  • To send transactional messages (welcome, login alerts, low stock, closing) when you enable them.
  • To meet Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Sharing

We share data only as needed:

  • Cashfree: name, mobile, email, amount, and order reference so they can collect your subscription.
  • Hosting and email: Hostinger (application hosting and SMTP) to run the Service and send mail from pos@atavtelecom.in.
  • Law: if a court, tax authority, or regulator lawfully requires it.

We do not sell personal data. Shop staff see only that shop’s data. Master Admin support staff may access a shop to fix billing or technical issues.

Retention and security

We keep shop data while the account is active. After deletion (see our Data Deletion Policy) operational data is removed from live systems, subject to backup windows and records we must keep for tax or dispute purposes. Passwords are hashed. Access is limited to authorised ATAV staff and your shop users.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing noc@atavtelecom.in. You may also export a shop backup from Settings (shop admin) or ask us to do so before deletion.

Children

ATAV POS is a business product. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 for subscriptions.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above will change. Continued use of the Service after an update means you accept the revised policy.
