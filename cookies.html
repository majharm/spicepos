Cookie Policy

Effective 3 September 2026 · Last updated 3 September 2026

This policy describes how ATAV POS uses cookies and similar storage in your browser. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

What we use

  • Essential session: a login / session cookie or token so you stay signed in while you use the shop or Master Admin. Without this the POS cannot work.
  • Local storage: shop UI state such as theme, last screen, bill slider preference, and “remember me” identifiers. This stays on your device.
  • Cashfree checkout: when you pay a subscription, Cashfree may set cookies on their payment page to complete UPI, card, or net-banking and to prevent fraud. Those cookies are controlled by Cashfree, not by ATAV.

What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party ad networks, or cross-site tracking pixels on the login or POS pages.

How long they last

Session cookies expire when you sign out or close the browser, unless you chose Remember me. Local storage remains until you clear site data. Cashfree cookie lifetimes follow Cashfree’s policy.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, you will not be able to sign in. Clearing site data signs you out and resets POS display preferences on that device.

Updates

If we add optional analytics later, we will update this page and the date above.
