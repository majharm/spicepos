How to use Open POS
  1. Sign in at /master.html
  2. Businesses → Open POS (enters as business admin)
     OR Users → Open POS (enters as that specific user)
  3. Shop POS opens with full access (even if expired/suspended)
  4. Back to Master Admin ends shop view only
