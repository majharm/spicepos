Data Deletion Policy

Effective 3 September 2026 · Last updated 3 September 2026

This policy explains how ATAV Telecom deletes personal and shop data from ATAV POS when you ask us to, when a trial or paid shop is closed, or when the law requires it.

How to request deletion

The shop owner (or an authorised person) should email noc@atavtelecom.in from the registered shop email, or WhatsApp / call +91 97650 40588, with:

  • Shop / business name and login email or username
  • Confirmation that you want the shop and personal data deleted
  • Whether you already downloaded a backup from shop Settings

We may ask you to verify identity (OTP, registered mobile, or GSTIN) before we delete a live shop.

What we delete

  • Shop profile, staff logins, items, customers, suppliers, bills, holds, purchases, expenses, and settings from the live database
  • Uploaded logos and item photos for that shop
  • Access tokens so nobody can sign in to that shop

When it happens

We complete live deletion within 30 days of a verified request. Encrypted backups and server snapshots may retain a copy for up to 90 days, after which they expire in the normal backup cycle.

What we may retain

  • Subscription invoices, GST records, and payment references required under Indian tax law (typically up to 8 years)
  • Records needed to defend a chargeback, fraud, or legal claim
  • Anonymised usage counts that cannot identify you

Cashfree holds payment-instrument data on their systems under their own policy. Ask Cashfree if you need deletion of instrument tokens they stored for checkout.

Account closure without a request

If a trial ends and no subscription is paid, or a paid shop stays suspended for a long period, we may close the shop and apply this same deletion timeline after notice to the registered email.

Staff and Master Admin

A shop admin can deactivate staff users inside the POS. Full shop deletion is done by ATAV support or Master Admin after the request above.
