Refund & Cancellation

Effective 3 September 2026 · Last updated 3 September 2026

ATAV POS is a digital software subscription. Payments for paid plans are collected by Cashfree Payments India Private Limited on behalf of ATAV Telecom.

Trial

New shops receive a 2-day free trial. No payment is required to start the trial. If you do not subscribe, the shop may be locked when the trial ends. There is nothing to refund for an unused trial.

Cancellation

You may cancel a paid subscription at any time by emailing noc@atavtelecom.in or calling +91 97650 40588. Cancellation stops the next renewal. You keep access until the period you already paid for ends, unless we agree a different date in writing.

Refunds

  • First paid invoice: if you have not used the POS in a meaningful way after paying (no sales bills of substance), you may request a refund within 7 days of the successful Cashfree payment.
  • After use or after 7 days: subscription fees are generally non-refundable for that billing period. We may issue a goodwill credit at our discretion (for example a duplicate charge or a failed activation).
  • Gateway fees: Cashfree or bank fees already deducted on a captured payment may be withheld from the refunded amount where the network does not reverse them.

How to request a refund

Write to noc@atavtelecom.in with shop name, login email, Cashfree / UTR reference, amount, and reason. We respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are sent back through Cashfree to the original payment method. Banks may take 5–10 business days to show the credit.

Failed payments and chargebacks

If Cashfree reports a failed or cancelled payment, we do not activate the paid period. Chargebacks raised with the bank are investigated; we may share shop invoices and login logs with Cashfree to contest misuse.

GST

Where GST was charged on a subscription invoice, any refund follows GST rules. You may receive a credit note instead of a cash refund where required.
