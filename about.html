Who we are

ATAV TELECOM started with a simple idea: Indian shops, restaurants, pharmacies and service businesses deserve software that is as serious as the work they do every day. From a Pune base we design, build and run products that sit at the centre of billing, stock, customers and communication.

We are not a generic website agency that also “does POS”. We stay close to the counter — GST invoices, barcode scan, weighing scales, KOT, expiry, loyalty, WhatsApp alerts — and we wrap that in cloud software the owner can open from a phone after closing time.

Our work spans five connected practices: IT services, custom software development, SaaS products such as ATAV POS, cloud communication (SMS, WhatsApp API, voice, RCS), and digital marketing that helps the same businesses get found and sell more.

How we work

We listen to the trade first — kirana, pharmacy, garment, café, wholesale — then map a stack that the staff will actually use. Implementation is practical: live till, training, and support on +91 97650 40588.

Growth is measured in faster bills, fewer stock-outs, repeat customers and reports the owner can trust. That is the “software backbone” we mean: the system that quietly runs the business while people serve people.

IT services & software

SaaS — ATAV POS

Billing, inventory, customers, payments, staff, multi-branch and reports in one login. Built for retail, pharmacy, F&B, salon, garment and more.
